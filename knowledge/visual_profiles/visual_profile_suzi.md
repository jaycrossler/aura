crowded station concourse — this is now the visual shorthand for "security" on

## What People Notice First

Fifty and in better shape than most people half her age on the station, worn
in but not worn down. Ten years in the job; plans to die on Fortuna, one of the
very few people there who isn't passing through. The blue-and-black uniform
should now be the first visual read in any scene — recognizable before her
face is.

## Body Language

- Trained for confrontation, never stopped expecting to need it.
- Most working hours spent standing between Fortuna's factions before an
  argument becomes something worse — a de-escalation posture, probably her
  most-used physical state.
- Patient, unhurried observation; looks at people longer than is comfortable
  and doesn't perform doing so.
