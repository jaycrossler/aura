# Astral Light-Speed Addendum

## The ruling

Light does not propagate at a constant speed *c* within the Astral. Instead,
**everything that moves in the Astral — photons, electrons, matter, people —
moves at a rate proportional to distance-squared (dist²), until it decoheres
or resets.** This is not a rule specific to light; light is simply the first
thing the team measured it through.

## Relationship to Chapter 24 (X-05a)

Chapter 24's preliminary finding — a physical range ratio of four maps to
about two in the Astral, nine maps to three, sixteen maps to four — was
correct as far as it went. It was an early, real measurement of the same
underlying law this addendum formalizes, not a mistaken interpretation later
overturned.

**This addendum COMPOUNDS with X-05a. It does not replace it.** The later
rotating-mirror experiment (X-10) doesn't correct Ch24's model — it explains
*why* the model holds: the compression the team measured isn't a distance
illusion or a camera artifact, it's a direct consequence of Astral motion
itself running on dist² rather than linear distance.

## What "until it decoheres or resets" means

Objects and energy moving through the Astral accumulate this dist² effect
continuously, but the effect isn't unbounded — it terminates at a decoherence
or reset event specific to the moving thing (the boundary conditions of
individual decoherence/reset events are not fully specified here and should
be worked out scene-by-scene as needed, rather than treated as a fixed
universal constant at this stage).

## What does NOT change

- **Existing travel-time math is confirmed unchanged.** Any dist²/dist³
  calculations already used elsewhere in the KB (e.g., Adama Station
  distances) remain valid. This ruling explains the mechanism; it does not
  require re-deriving results already established.
- The characters' epistemic caution in Ch24 ("consistent with a square," not
  a proven law) remains correct in-story — they don't get the full dist²
  law confirmed until the rotating-mirror experiment (X-10).

## Story implications going forward

- `X-10` (rotating-mirror experiment) can now be drafted as a real physics
  beat rather than a placeholder — it's the scene where Sophia/Brandon/{Aura}
  confirm the dist² law directly, rather than inferring it from star
  positions.
- This gives mechanical grounding for the new vehicle/platinum-survey thread
  (see `arcs/arc_01_thread_V_vehicles_and_platinum_survey.md`): fast
  Astral-side travel between asteroids is physically real, not a hand-wave,
  because objects moving through the Astral are covering physical-space
  distance at a compressed rate.
