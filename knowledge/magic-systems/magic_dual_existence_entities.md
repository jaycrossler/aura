# Dual-Existence Entities

## Concept

Most dimensional fauna exist primarily in the Astral and cross into physical
space as a discrete, resource-costly act (see [[magic_breach_crossers_transient]]).
A dual-existence entity does not cross — it is **anchored across both realms at
once**, with a single Template expressed simultaneously in both. Her physical-realm
body and her Astral-realm body are the same entity, not a projection and a source.

This is rare and should stay rare — a single-digit number of species across the
whole series, reserved for apex-tier threats where the "boss fight in one realm"
structure would otherwise be too easy for a party with mixed Astral/physical
capability.

## The Shield Mechanic

A dual-existence entity's Astral-side Template generates a **material-complexity
filter** — a shield that does not stop force, does not stop mass, and does not
stop speed. It stops *refinement*.

**What passes the filter:** unworked or minimally-shaped natural, single-material
substances — raw wood, stone, bone, unmodified plant fiber. Shape does not
disqualify an object (a carved or sharpened wood point still passes); *compositing
and refinement* do.

**What the filter stops:** metal (worked, alloyed, or raw ore past a smelting
threshold), synthetic materials, composites, powered devices, and anything
assembled from multiple distinct refined components — swords, bullets, drone
housings, force-constructed weapons, Kael's iron bracer, Force Application
constructs built from complex Templates.

**Why this works narratively:** it is not a power-level gate (Kael's raw
Will-force fails exactly like a bullet does — the filter doesn't care how
strong the attacker is), it is a *material* gate. This keeps the solution
discoverable through observation rather than escalating power, which fits
the series' anti-"power-creep-solves-everything" ethos.

**Physical-realm consequence:** because her Astral Template and physical body
are the same entity, the shield's nullification applies in physical space too.
Fire, explosives, and conventional weapons striking her physical form do
negligible or no damage — she is, in effect, ignoring most physical-realm
attacks entirely and treating the physical-space fight as a minor irritant
while her real vulnerability sits in the Astral.

## Discovery Beat (S-01, Stage 3)

During the fight, **someone throws a rock** at the Queen — reflexive, out of
options, not a plan. It passes clean through the shield where every prepared
weapon has failed. This is witnessed by Sophia. She has, at that moment, one
unrefined wood object on her person: **the pen blank her father carved for
her** (see [[char_sophia_lotte]]). She strips the ink mechanism out — the only
part of the pen that was ever "manufactured" — leaving pure worked wood, and
launches it with everything she has.

*Must accomplish when drafted:* the rock-throw should read as someone's
desperate, almost embarrassing improvisation, not a eureka moment — the
eureka belongs to Sophia, watching it happen and immediately understanding
why. This should be fast, a handful of lines, not a lexical lecture.

## Boundary Cases (ruled, for future consistency)

- Bone: passes (natural, unrefined).
- Raw ore: does not pass once smelted/alloyed; unworked ore is a gray area —
  default to **fails** (most "raw ore" a character would have on hand has
  already been processed).
- A wood-handled tool with a metal head: fails (composite).
- Kael's sword: fails (worked metal).
- Kael's raw Will-force strikes: fails (not a material at all, but the filter
  reads directed Will-constructs the same as refined matter — this is
  deliberate; otherwise the filter would be trivially bypassed by any
  practitioner).
