# Thread V — Vehicles and the Platinum Survey

## Status note

Scene contracts drafted and chapter placement resolved (2026-08-21). V-01 opens Chapter 27.

## Placement

V-01 opens Chapter 27, directly after Chapter 26 ("The Sweep"). X-05b ("The Unstable Site") is deferred to a later chapter — no longer assumed to precede this thread. Overlaps with the tail end of Thread K (Kael's covert departure/return window).

## Physics grounding

This thread is now mechanically justified by the Astral light-speed ruling
(see `universe-spec/spec_astral_lightspeed_addendum.md`): motion through the
Astral covers physical-space distance at a compressed (dist²) rate, which is
*why* Astral-side exploration is meaningfully faster than physical-space
travel, not just a convenient escalation.

## Assumptions made while drafting

- **Rocket scale:** a two-to-three-person scout craft, repurposed from existing
  Fortuna survey-shuttle hardware rather than built from scratch — fits Origin's
  budget posture and keeps this from becoming its own sub-plot about procurement.
- **Sparky's autonomy:** bounded, timed solo windows (not open-ended unsupervised
  operation), consistent with the established crossing-cost/exhaustion rules —
  Jace or Sophia set a hard return time before each run.
- **The find:** an escalating search — several unproductive runs before the
  platinum strike — rather than a lucky first hit, so the eventual find reads as
  earned and the explosions (V-05) have time to accumulate as a pattern rather
  than a single event.
- **Cover story reach:** Kim is looped in at a "verifies the survey paperwork,
  asks no questions she doesn't want answered" level; Carlos handles the Origin
  finance side. Suzi is deliberately NOT looped in — consistent with her ongoing
  Helena investigation staying separate from this thread.
- **Materials-spec tie-in:** per `open_questions_materials_spec_v1.md`, platinum's
  Group 11 membership gives the deposit a faint traversal cost even before anyone
  understands why — Sparky's approach runs measurably slower near the asteroid,
  which becomes a clue rather than an explained mechanism (matches the Arc 1
  opacity rule).

## Sequence

### V-01 — The Rocket

**Summary:** With Sophia's room now a functioning field station (Ch24) and
{Aura-S} operational in Sparky, the team faces a hard limit: everything they've
learned about Astral-side speed is useless without a physical-space vehicle fast
enough to reach and recover assets at the distances involved. Jace, drawing on
his engineering background, leads a retrofit of an existing Origin survey shuttle
into a longer-range scout craft. Brandon handles propellant chemistry; Nick
handles the instrumentation stack.

**Must accomplish:**
1. The rocket is a retrofit of existing station hardware, not a from-scratch
   build — this keeps Origin paperwork involved without making procurement the
   plot.
2. Jace's engineering competence gets one clean, uncomplicated win here — no
   crisis, no cost, just him being good at something in front of people who've
   only seen him as erratic lately.
3. The craft is explicitly built to give Sparky's Astral runs a physical-space
   anchor and recovery point, not to replace the Astral exploration itself.
4. Establish who can fly/crew it (Jace confirmed; leave open whether Brandon or
   Nikos also qualify) without resolving it as a subplot.

**Must NOT:**
- Turn this into a heist or unauthorized-requisition story — Carlos signs off,
  on the record, framed as legitimate survey-capability investment.
- Give the rocket combat capability or implications yet; it's a scout/recovery
  vehicle.

---

### V-02 — Sparky's Asteroid Runs

**Summary:** With the rocket as a physical-space anchor, Sparky begins a series
of bounded, timed solo runs into the Astral to scan nearby asteroids for
resources — ground physically inaccessible at this speed any other way. The
early runs are unproductive: interesting formations, nothing valuable, one run
cut short when Sparky's return window nearly lapses and Jace has a bad ten
minutes waiting.

**Must accomplish:**
1. Each run has a hard, pre-set return time — the crossing-cost/exhaustion rules
   apply to Sparky's {Aura-S} instance too, not just to human crossers.
2. At least one early run goes wrong in a small, recoverable way (the near-miss
   return window) to keep the stakes real before the payoff in V-03.
3. The runs read as real exploration — new sights, real astronomy, a growing
   informal star/asteroid catalog Nikos gets excited about — not just montage.
4. Nick's {Aura-S} telemetry from these runs is what eventually lets him
   cross-reference Will-resonance patterns (seeds N-02's three-minute warning
   system).

**Must NOT:**
- Make the search feel effortless — several dry runs are load-bearing for the
  "earned find" texture the author asked for.

---

### V-03 — The Find

**Summary:** On a later run, Sparky's approach to one asteroid slows measurably
more than the crossing-cost model predicts for its mass — the team's first hint
that something about this rock interacts with Astral traversal differently.
Deeper scanning reveals a substantial platinum-group deposit. No one names the
mechanism; Sophia logs the anomalous approach-speed data without an explanation,
consistent with the project's rule that Group 11 effects stay unexplained
on-page in Arc 1.

**Must accomplish:**
1. The anomalous slowdown is presented as a data point, not a solved mystery —
   Sophia notes it, no one explains it.
2. The find itself should land as a genuine "everyone stops talking" beat after
   several dry runs.
3. Establish the deposit's approximate scale in a way Carlos/Kim can later
   translate into real station-finance stakes (V-04).

**Must NOT:**
- Have any character articulate an atomic-number or electron-sea explanation
  for the slowdown — matches the existing reveal-control rule for silver/copper.

---

### V-04 — The Cover Story

**Summary:** The team launders the discovery into a conventional find. Kim
signs off on updated survey routing that "happens" to pass near the asteroid;
Carlos handles the Origin-facing paperwork treating it as a standard prospecting
success. Neither is told how the deposit was actually located — both are given
just enough to make the paperwork true without being lies.

**Must accomplish:**
1. Kim's involvement is narrow and deliberate — she verifies routing and
   asks no questions she doesn't want answered, consistent with her established
   "attention over shame" management style.
2. Carlos processes the finance side with his usual quiet competence; his
   established cardigan-and-tie steadiness makes him the right character to
   sell this to Origin without anyone flagging it.
3. Show one concrete effect on station finance or morale (e.g., a policy Carlos
   mentioned earlier — token economy, ration status — visibly eases), so the
   discovery has stakes beyond plot mechanics.
4. Suzi is not looped in. Her Helena investigation stays on its own track.

**Must NOT:**
- Let the cover story read as effortless or risk-free — someone (Kim, most
  plausibly) should get close enough to the truth to make Jace sweat, without
  actually learning it.

---

### V-05 — The Explosions

**Summary:** Continued testing — rocket engine iteration, Sparky's repeated
Astral-side operations, or both — produces a series of large explosions in the
Astral. This is the attention-drawing event: both Kael (already in-system per
Thread K) and the Scavenging Queen register it as the brightest, most
significant Will-expenditure activity Fortuna has produced, triggering Kael's
return and the swarm's approach.

**Must accomplish:**
1. The explosions read as the natural cost of ambitious, under-supervised
   experimentation moving fast — not a blameable mistake.
2. At least one explosion should have a near-miss human cost (injury, close
   call) to keep the stakes physical, not just narratively symbolic.
3. Function explicitly as the direct causal bridge into Thread S — Kael's
   return belongs to Thread S's lead-in (K-08, not yet written) as established.
4. Requires Thread K's K-06 (Kael's departure) to already have happened.

**Must NOT:**
- Resolve or explain what the Scavenging Queen "senses" beyond "the brightest
  Will event yet" — the Queen's interiority stays opaque until Thread S.
