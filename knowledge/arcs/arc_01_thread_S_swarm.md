**Stage 3 — The Queen (RULED 2026-07-28 — dual-existence, corrected mechanic).**
The Scavenging Queen arrives at the deepest breach point. Sophia freezes on
contact — she has never seen a Will-signature like this — and stays anyway;
the freeze recontextualizes her final positioning as chosen courage, not
absence of fear.

**Mechanic (see [[magic_dual_existence_entities]]):** the Queen exists
simultaneously in the Astral and physical realms. Her Astral shield nullifies
any refined/composite material or directed Will-construct — swords, bullets,
force-spikes, explosives — in *both* realms at once, **for as long as the
shield holds.** Physical-realm attacks against her do negligible real damage
while the shield is up.

This is a **four-source convergence — three of suppression, one kill, and a
finisher**, not four sources of simultaneous damage:
- **Jace** uses explosives against the Queen — while the shield holds, this
  does not meaningfully hurt her, but the blast/concussion and continuous
  threat force her to keep reacting to the physical-space fight rather than
  focus fully on the Astral.
- **Jin's remaining miners** (Shi Gang, Li Hao) fight with fire — same
  function while the shield holds: pressure and distraction, not damage.
- **Kael**, present under occultation the entire fight (per the Closing
  Condition / S-02b), engages the Queen's minions in the Astral — clearing
  the vanguard so the Queen can't simply retreat behind a wall of
  reinforcements. Kael does not engage the Queen directly and does not know
  about the material-shield rule going in.
- **The discovery beat and the coup de grâce:** amid the chaos, someone —
  desperate, out of options — throws a rock at the Queen. It passes clean
  through the shield where every prepared weapon has failed. Sophia sees it
  happen and immediately understands why (see [[char_sophia_lotte]]):
  unrefined material bypasses the filter. She has one such object on her —
  her father's wood pen. She strips the ink mechanism out, leaving bare
  worked wood, and channels everything she has into it: a Force output
  equivalent to launching a boulder, driving the wood shaft through the
  shield and through the Queen's eye into its brain. "Never piss off a
  physicist." **The kill drops the shield.** In the same breath, Jace's
  explosives and the miners' fire — already in contact or already
  airborne, no longer suppressing a shielded target — land for real and
  finish her: fire and detonation against a now-undefended physical body.
  Sophia's shot is the kill; the fire and explosions are what visibly ends
  her on screen.

**Kael's reaction (must accomplish, shown on-page):** Kael clears the
vanguard first — fighting a dozen-plus cryptids at once, sword and
improvised force-spikes punching through armored shells — because she's
read the Queen's position (anchored to the station's hull, about to tear it
open) and knows the vanguard has to fall before anyone else can act. Once
clear, she pulls in Will, grows, glows, and launches herself along conjured
force-footholds to match the station's rotation and land near the Queen's
position — a "comet of retribution" beat. She bellows a challenge.

**And then nothing happens.** The Queen is already still — Sophia's kill and
the finishing fire/explosions land first. The humans below are staring up at
Kael, not at the corpse, because the corpse is old news to them and she's
the new variable. Her surprise is external and physical (the pause, the
head-turn, the re-survey of the scene) rather than narrated internally — she
doesn't have English to think it in yet, and the reader should feel her
recalculating in real time: *how are they still alive, what did they do.*

**Kael had written the station off (must accomplish).** Going into this
fight, Kael's private assessment — never stated aloud to the reader before
now, revealed retroactively through this beat — was that Fortuna would not
survive intact; her plan was damage control and extraction of anything worth
saving, not victory. She was wrong, and this lands as a real, specific
realization, not vague awe: the humans she'd assessed as barely-armed and
underdeveloped organized themselves, suppressed an entity she considers
genuinely dangerous, and one of them improvised a kill Kael herself would
not have found without months of study. This is the seed of her Arc 2
respect arc — not "humans are surprisingly strong" in the abstract, but "I
specifically underestimated these people and it nearly cost me the right
read."

This is a plant for Arc 2, not resolved here.

*Illustrative tone (not final prose, for the drafter's calibration):*
> She takes on a dozen of the cryptid monsters, sword flailing and precision
> strikes as she punches through their armored shells with spikes of
> ethereal force... After she stops the vanguard, she turns and pulls in all
> the Will she can, glowing, sparking, growing in size... launches herself up
> and matches her speed to the station's edge... She bellows a challenge...
> And pauses. It is completely still. The small humans in their metal suits
> are standing around it, poking at it but staring up at her in shock and
> awe. Her head turns, surveying the scene. It still isn't moving. How are
> they still alive? What did they do?

**Scale/tone note for the Queen throughout Stage 3 (from Jace's POV):** the
Queen should read as a small *structure*, not a big animal — weight that
lists the deck when it slams down, claws that screech on the metal. Ground
every Jace-POV beat in mundane physical consequence (deck tilt, sound,
his own smallness against it) rather than abstract scale description.

*Must accomplish:* the kill is a convergence — Jace and the miners suppress
while the shield holds, then finish her once it drops; Kael clears the
field; an anonymous desperate rock-throw is the actual discovery; Sophia
executes the coup de grâce; Kael is present, effective against minions, and
explicitly not the deciding factor; Kael's private "written off the
station" assessment and her realization she was wrong are shown.

*Must NOT do:* let Jace's explosives or the miners' fire land as real damage
*before* Sophia's kill — they must read as pressure/distraction until the
shield drops, then as the real finishing blow; let Kael's involvement read
as her carrying the fight; let the rock-throw read as a clever plan rather
than a desperate reflex that Sophia alone recognizes the significance of;
let Kael's "I was wrong" beat be generic awe rather than a specific,
consequential recalibration of her assessment.

#### S-02 — After

**Summary:** Jin comes through after the mop-up is confirmed. He looks at Sophia.
He says something in Mandarin. His {Aura} translates, with a brief lag: *"You
killed a very large spider."* Sophia says: *"Yes."* Jin says: *"Good."* This is
the beginning of a friendship.

Later, talking to Jace while they wait for medical updates, Jin mentions the
three months of nest-clearing. Eleven nests. Drill lances. His crew's hands.
Jace's reaction is the reader's reaction.

2. Jin/Sophia exchange is the emotional pivot of the aftermath
3. Nick's aftermath is shown without drama — he crossed against every instinct
   and then sat on the floor; this is honorable
4. The nest-clearing revelation happens here, in conversation, as context

#### S-02b — The Planning Session / Kael Revealed

**Summary:** Nick's {Aura} module produces the alert — three minutes of tactical
warning, plus a longer build-up window giving approximately two hours for
planning. The planning session is the first time Jace, Sophia, Jin, and Nick are
in the same room, and the first time Jace and Sophia tell the station's defenders
what they have been doing.

The chaos is real. Sophia pushes an object across a table without touching it.
Jin's crew does not look surprised — they look like people for whom a
long-running mystery has just been named. Li Hao says something in Mandarin. Shi
Gang says nothing.

During the session: Jace mentions, a few times, the woman standing near him. He
mentions her conversationally, as you mention someone in a crowded room. *"The
woman by the door might have a better vantage point."* He stops. No one is
looking where he indicated. He mentions it two more times. Three total. Each
time, the room responds to everything he says except the reference to her.

{Penny} has been sending Jace unauthorized-intrusion alerts. He has been
silencing them. Nine in forty minutes. The entry points are moving.

Sophia notices Jace's expression during the third alert he silences. She crosses
into the Astral.

**What Sophia sees:** The woman is there. In the Astral she is brilliant — not
warm-bright, brilliant the way that is several orders of magnitude removed from
everyone else in the room. Sophia has never seen a Will-signature like this. She
is reaching into Jace's Template. Sophia sees the egg — sees it for the first
time, clear and horrible, lodged there since X-02a — and sees the woman remove
it. Clean, immediate, complete. In physical space, Jace blinks. He says something
to {Aura} that doesn't connect to anything anyone said. He looks like someone who
has come in from somewhere very cold. Then he shakes his head and pays attention.

AH Level drops from 4.5 to 0. Jace does not know what changed.

**Must accomplish:**
- Kael is in the planning session before anyone knows she is there
- Sophia tears the occultation ("Mind Web") with an improvised force pull — her
  plan to continue observing undetected ends there
- Kael's absence of alarm is what makes her frightening, not her capability
- Kael cannot understand English; no one can understand her; zero vocabulary at
  Arc 1 close — the translation project is Arc 2 material
