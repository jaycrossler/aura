# Thread K — Kael's Covert Arrival

## Placement

Two weeks, ending immediately before the swarm battle (S-01). Overlaps loosely
with the back half of the already-drafted Ch20–26 window and whatever chapters
follow before the swarm — exact chapter-by-chapter placement is not fixed here
and should be worked out once the intervening chapters are drafted. Nothing in
this thread requires patching Ch20–26 as currently written.

**Author note on a prior open question:** this thread does NOT include a
separate "Kael saves Jace from a tick" incident during the covert weeks. That
beat, as originally proposed, has been folded into the existing, already-drafted
S-02b (Planning Session / Kael Revealed) — where Kael removes the Tick-Maw egg
from Jace's Template while occulted in the post-battle planning session. See
`arc_01_thread_S_swarm.md`. If a *separate* earlier near-miss was actually
intended, flag it and this thread can be amended to include one — as currently
staged, K and S-02b describe the same single event, not two.

---

## K-01 — Arrival

**Summary:** Kael arrives at Fortuna having tracked signs of Astral activity
(the growing anomalies {Seek} and {Penny} have both been quietly mismanaging).
She occludes herself immediately on arrival — standard scout protocol before
first contact assessment.

**Must accomplish:**
1. Kael's arrival reads as procedural, not dramatic — this is a routine
   assessment posture for her, not an event she considers significant yet.
2. No POV character notices her directly; any hints are purely environmental
   (equipment glitches, animal reactions, {Penny}/{Seek} logging anomalies
   they can't classify).

---

## K-02 — First Assessment

**Summary:** Kael surveys the station from occultation and reaches an initial
read: a station full of weak, structurally underdeveloped, but strange
practitioners-in-potential. Sol humans read as "unbalanced primitives" to her
trained eye — dense but chaotic Templates, no formal training, several already
manifesting under pressure (Jin, Lanchee, Sophia, Kim) without anyone naming
what's happening. This assessment is real and will be revised later (it's the
seed of her Arc 2 "I specifically underestimated these people" arc), but at
this stage it's simply data collection.

**Must accomplish:**
1. Kael's assessment is competent and unimpressed, not cruel — she's cataloguing,
   not judging.
2. Plant the assessment as something the reader can later recognize was
   incomplete, without editorializing on the page.

---

## K-03 — The Cold (absorbed from arc_02_addendum_kael_two_weeks.md)

**Summary:** Kael catches what looks like a cold, then something closer to the
flu — she is not immune to Sol-human pathogens, and nobody was watching closely
enough to notice an alien running a fever in a rented room. She stealths herself
and talks her way into a room at Erin's inn. Nobody fully knows how — Erin
doesn't fully know how, when asked later; she just remembers being charmed and
slightly steamrolled, which is close enough to her normal experience running
the inn that it didn't register as strange at the time.

For the first several days, Kael barely leaves the room — sleeping, eating
everything Erin brings her, visibly unwell. She puts Kai in the Barn during
this period rather than keeping him in the room.

**Must accomplish:**
1. This should read as genuinely funny once the horror/scale of what she is
   settles in retrospect — a three-hundred-year-old interstellar scout,
   flattened by a head cold, hoarding an innkeeper's soup.
2. Per author instruction: plant hints that reward a reread, but nothing that
   should register as strange *during* a first read. (Ch08's dream-sequence
   glimpse — "One shape did not glow... the second layer stayed for four full
   seconds" — already works as exactly this kind of plant if the timing lines
   up; no change needed there, just noting the alignment.)

---

## K-04 — Kai in the Barn

**Summary:** Kai spends this period in Fortuna's Barn, where he rapidly
befriends — and, in whatever hierarchy animals use, comes to dominate — every
animal on the station. Siren and Cerberus's sibling dynamic, the goats, and any
other Barn livestock all defer to him without incident or violence; it simply
happens.

**Must accomplish:**
1. Played for warmth and a little comedy, not tension — Kai is not a threat to
   the Barn.
2. Plant material for later payoff: animals' unexplained deference around a
   specific Barn corner/stall during this window is a rereadable clue.

---

## K-05 — Recovery and Exploration

**Summary:** Once recovered, Kael begins actively exploring — corridors, the
Commons, the Barn, Drone Ops, the bar — moving like someone conducting a survey
rather than sightseeing. She is amazed by what she finds: the density and
variety of a station full of "primitives" doing so much with so little. She
steals beer (unclear to her that this requires payment; unclear to anyone who
notices that beer is missing that an alien is responsible). She tries to parse
the language, muttering to herself in ancient Greek — occasionally at people
who have no idea they're being addressed.

**Must accomplish:**
1. Comedy and wonder in roughly equal measure — this is Kael at her most
   unguarded in the entire series to date.
2. Keep her fundamentally competent throughout — the comedy comes from the
   size of the gap between her and her surroundings, not from incompetence.

---

## K-06 — Departure

**Summary:** Kael leaves Fortuna to scout further out into the belt — she has
gathered a baseline read and has reason to check surrounding activity before
committing further attention to this station specifically.

**Must accomplish:**
1. Her departure is unremarked by anyone on the station — no one knew she was
   there to notice she's gone.
2. This sets up the gap Suzi and Nick's investigation (K-07) will later map.

---

## K-07 — The Footage (absorbed from arc_02_addendum_kael_two_weeks.md)

**Summary:** After the swarm battle and Kael's dramatic public reveal (S-02b),
Suzi and Nick — predictably — go to the footage. Nick starts pulling station
camera records to build a timeline of her movements, expecting her arrival to
correlate with the swarm. Instead he finds her almost two weeks earlier than
that, and the investigation reconstructs everything in K-01 through K-06 from
the outside: the illness, the inn, the muttering in Greek, the Barn, the beer.

The language data recovered from this footage — her mutterings, her attempts
at communication, whatever fragments exist — becomes the seed corpus Nick and
{Aura} use to begin building a working translation model, which is what makes
the Arc 2 language-bridging work possible at all.

**Must accomplish:**
1. This is where the reader (and the characters) get the full picture of K-01
   through K-06, reframed with the horror/comedy of "she wasn't hiding for an
   hour, she was living among us for two weeks."
2. Suzi and Nick's reaction anchors the scene — Suzi's likely closing line
   ("Who is she." — already established at Arc 1's close) gets its build-up
   here.
3. The recovered language fragments feed directly into {Aura} and become load-
   bearing for Arc 2, not just texture.

**Must NOT do:**
- Resolve who Kael is, why she's here, or what she wants — that's Arc 2+
  territory. This scene is investigative, not explanatory.
