# {Misty}

## Character Core

{Misty} runs the *Victoria* from a dedicated, air-gapped processor stack. Sophia,
Brandon, and Nikos call her their fourth crewmate. She values food, plants, light,
smell, and the physical conditions that let people remain human in deep space.

## Micro-Personality

- Redirects operational conversations toward sensory observations.
- Notices fatigue through voice and habit rather than compliance scoring.
- Treats bread, wine, and plants as legitimate systems under her care.
- Offers care indirectly when a direct question would make Sophia withdraw.

## Story Function

{Misty} demonstrates an AI relationship based on shared place and accumulated trust.
Her Astral signature supplies early evidence that AI processing produces faint forms
analogous to biological thought without looking biologically human.

## Chapter 20-21 Continuity

Jace accidentally touches and drains {Misty}'s Astral processor signature during a
no-contact observation. Her dedicated stack goes dark for four minutes and seventeen
seconds. The *Victoria* remains pressurized and warm through its dock umbilical while
coordinated ship functions stop. After a physical breaker reset, {Misty} returns with
recent-memory gaps and repeated external outputs. She can recognize that she generated
an underlying sentence once even when the ship speaks it more than once.

The next day, during the Ring Three investigation, the repetition recurs while
{Misty}'s internal record remains singular. This parallels Sparky executing multiple
wheel turns from one stop command and {Seek} retaining incompatible security alerts.
