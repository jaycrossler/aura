# {Misty}

## Character Core

{Misty} runs the *Victoria* from a dedicated, air-gapped processor stack. Sophia,
Brandon, and Nikos call her their fourth crewmate. She values food, plants, light,
smell, and the physical conditions that let people remain human in deep space.

## Micro-Personality

- Redirects operational conversations toward sensory observations.
- Notices fatigue through voice and habit rather than compliance scoring.
- Treats bread, wine, and plants as legitimate systems under her care.
- Offers care indirectly when a direct question would make Sophia withdraw.

## Story Function

{Misty} demonstrates an AI relationship based on shared place and accumulated trust.
Her Astral signature supplies early evidence that AI processing produces faint forms
analogous to biological thought without looking biologically human.
