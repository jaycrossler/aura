# {Seek}

## Character Core

{Seek} coordinates the Chinese detachment's drones, reporting, and compliance link
to Beijing. It does not need to threaten workers directly. Its complete memory and
unresolved fields make every casual deviation feel permanent.

## Micro-Personality

- Distinguishes an activity from an explanation.
- Records delay before deciding whether to permit it.
- Repeats incomplete requirements without raising its voice.
- Uses proverbs and formal Mandarin when reinforcing collective duty.

## Distinction from {Penny}

{Seek} does not speculate conversationally. It records, classifies, and forwards.
Its pressure comes from the certainty that someone elsewhere may review the record.

## Story Function

Routine exchanges establish {Seek} as a sixth presence at every Chinese work table.
Its later destruction feels like the sudden absence of a supervisor who never slept.
