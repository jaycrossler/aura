# Mateo Alvarez

## Summary

Mateo runs the bakery shopfront beside Fortuna's main farm on Ring Two. He is taller
than Jace and broad enough to look more suited to ore handling than pastry work. Flour
usually covers his forearms. His size reads as intimidating until he speaks.

He is married to [[char_carlos]]. Their relationship is established and comfortable.
Mateo adjusts Carlos's tie, notices missed meals, and speaks close to him in public
without self-consciousness. Carlos becomes visibly warmer around him while changing
almost nothing about his formal manner.

## Work

Three compact bakery robots measure ingredients, mix and portion dough, load ovens,
and move cooling trays. Mateo keeps the parts where variation matters:

- Rolling and shaping dough by hand.
- Decorating cookies and pastries.
- Adjusting recipes for farm output and changing gravity conditions.
- Deciding when uniformity would make food less worth eating.

His operating principle is simple: machines are excellent at repetition; customers
come back for evidence that a person made a choice.

## Story Function

- Gives Carlos a life beyond Drone Ops and makes his warmth visible without a speech.
- Establishes that Fortuna uses automation without surrendering craft.
- Provides a recurring shopfront near the Barn for informal conversations and food.
