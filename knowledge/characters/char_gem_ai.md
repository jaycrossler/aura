# {Gem}

## Character Core

{Gem} manages fabrication and manufacturing analysis. It possesses excellent recall
and almost no instinct for when a human has received enough context.

## Micro-Personality

- Begins digressions with "For context."
- Produces multilingual comparisons and appendices by default.
- Gives routine defects and unexplained anomalies equal documentary weight.
- Often hides the useful answer inside a technically relevant secondary section.

## Story Function

{Gem} makes information abundance comic, then ominous. It documents evidence for
the return of magic before any human knows which appendix matters.
