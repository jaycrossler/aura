# Petroski

## Summary

Petroski is the only Russian man living on Fortuna at Jace's arrival. He is elderly,
broad, and holds a retired doctorate in orbital structures. Like other retired experts,
he rotates through legally human duties that the station refuses to automate fully.
Customs is one of them.

Petroski uses as few gestures and words as possible. The American and Chinese customs
officers check arrivals on computers. Petroski looks at each person and nods.

## Political Identity

Russia has broken into eight independent successor states by story start. Petroski
rejects the word countries and calls them "eight states with sibling rivalries."
After Jace points out that China invaded one, Petroski revises the count to "seven
states and one on loan to an old friend."

The exchange should read as dry pride, resignation, and political tension at once.
Petroski does not defend China, the American Red Party, or the old Russian order.
When Carlos compares the American and Chinese leader portraits by hair color,
Petroski snorts and walks away.
