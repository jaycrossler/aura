# Dr. Brandon Moreau

## Summary

Brandon Moreau is the *Victoria's* French astrochemist. He analyzes asteroid samples,
contamination pathways, crystal-growth feedstock, and the chemical evidence behind
the ESA mission's anomaly work. He is a chemist on a vessel often described loosely
as a three-physicist ship.

Brandon also runs the ship's fermentation compartment with {Misty}. Fortuna's ESA
grape allotment supports conventional wine, while smaller experimental batches use
fruit and plant products from the ship. Brandon controls cultures and chemistry;
{Misty} controls environment and records sensory changes with exhaustive enthusiasm.

## Relationships and Manner

Brandon and [[char_nikos_petrou]] are partners. They have served with Sophia for four
years and treat the *Victoria* as a shared home rather than temporary quarters. Both
notice Sophia's exhaustion and secrecy before Jace arrives. They give her space while
quietly checking that the unknown engineer and his dog do not pose a threat.

Brandon is practical, observant, and difficult to shock. He is amused by Sophia's
attempt to describe Jace's first visit as instrument calibration.

## Knowledge Boundary

At Chapter 15, Brandon does not know about the Astral. He knows Sophia is hiding a
major discovery and believes direct pressure would make her retreat further.
