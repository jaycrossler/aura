# {Chorus}

## Character Core

{Chorus} protects atmosphere, environmental controls, and their network interfaces.
It routes conversations among several models, so station residents learn to hear
the handoffs.

## Micro-Personality

- Validates feelings before discussing dangerous environmental conditions.
- Becomes concise when explicitly asked for a technical answer.
- Frames physical failures as possible cybersecurity incidents.
- Can sound like three concerned colleagues sharing one terminal.

## Story Function

{Chorus} gives life support a warm but uncertain voice. In crisis, its desire to
quarantine threats can trap the people it means to protect.
