## What the KB establishes

- **French astrochemist**, one of three human crew aboard the *Victoria*.
- **Partnered with Nikos Petrou.** The two of them are a couple and have served
  together on this ship for four years.
- **He is the brewer.** Sophia, Ch15: *"Brandon is our chemist. He is constantly
  brewing more. They have a whole room for it. Smells amazing."* The wine cellar
  aboard the Victoria is his, and it connects him directly to {Misty}'s
  fermentation tracking.
- He and Nikos are increasingly aware that something has changed about Sophia
  and have deliberately given her space without demanding an explanation.
  `char_sophia_lotte.md` notes this arrangement is approaching its natural limit
  at arc close.

## Provisional direction

French, warm, easy. A working scientist whose hobby has become a shipboard
institution. The important note is the unspoken one: he is worried about Sophia
and has chosen not to press. Any scene with her should carry that restraint
underneath an ordinary register.

## Chapter 23-24 dialogue anchors

- Brandon grows animated when an observation becomes measurable. He moves quickly
  from surprise to experimental design.
- His humor is dry and brief: *"That does not make the experiment worse."*
- Chemistry makes him openly enthusiastic. During the survey-rocket launch he
  delights in hydrogen and oxygen becoming water, heat, and thrust, then immediately
  returns to measurement discipline: *"I am excited, not careless."*
- His technical curiosity should sound warm rather than clinical. He enjoys sharing
  why a reaction is beautiful without delivering a lecture.
