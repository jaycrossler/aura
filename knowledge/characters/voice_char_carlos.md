# Character Voice Profile — Carlos

## Vocal Characteristics

### Audio Profile
- **Voice type:** Baritone — unhurried, low, comfortable
- **Accent:** General American, unremarkable and settled — nothing about his
  speech calls attention to a specific origin; he reads as someone who has
  lived on Fortuna long enough that his voice belongs to the station now
- **Pitch:** Medium-low, very even
- **Speech rate:** Deliberately slow — the pace of someone who has never once
  felt rushed and doesn't intend to start
- **Volume:** Moderate, conversational — never needs to project
- **Resonance:** Full chest resonance, comfortable
- **Distinctive features:** Delivers information in the order that matters,
  not the order he thought of it — a tour-guide's instinct for pacing applied
  to ordinary conversation; will let a silence sit rather than fill it

**Casting note:** An older, settled male voice — think a knowledgeable docent
or a favorite uncle who never seems to be in a hurry. Should sound
fundamentally *safe* to a nervous new arrival; the vocal opposite of
corporate onboarding energy.

### Vocal Variations by Emotional State

**Default (guide/host mode):**
- Warm but not effusive; measured; clearly enjoys the role without
  performing enjoyment
- Sample: *"You'll be wanting rescuing."*

**Dry humor:**
- Delivered completely flat — no vocal wink, the joke is entirely in the
  content
- Sample: *"I have the great honor of showing you around before Kim gets
  hold of your training schedule."*

**The one beat of hesitation (Ch07, Helena's jacket):**
- *"For the first time all day, the unhurried man took a moment."* This is
  the single vocal deviation in his profile so far — a real pause, longer
  than his usual conversational rhythm, before answering. Should read as
  weighing how much to say, not as reluctance to speak at all.
- Sample: *"Helena's."* — delivered short, flat, none of his usual
  expansiveness; the contrast with his normal register is the point.

## Speech Patterns

### Verbal Tics and Habits
- States practical information plainly, without hedging ("Twenty minutes, if
  nobody does.")
- Rarely asks questions — states, offers, or explains
- Uses precise, slightly formal phrasing even in casual contexts ("I have the
  great honor of..." rather than "I get to...")

### Vocabulary Range
- **Technical:** Fluent in drone-ops and station-logistics terms, delivered
  plainly without jargon-showing
- **Emotional:** Restrained — the Helena beat is the clearest example of
  emotional content, and even there the delivery stays understated
- **Casual:** Dry, economical, comfortable with silence

### Relationship-Specific Speech

**With Jace (new arrival under his sponsorship):** Welcoming, informative,
protective in an unfussy way — the "rescuing" line establishes he sees his
role partly as running interference between Jace and the more overwhelming
parts of arrival day.

**With Cerberus:** No spoken dialogue, but a notable non-verbal beat worth
flagging for a director: he "does not coo," extends a hand at dog height,
waits to be sniffed, and nods "as between professionals" — if this is voiced
at all (a murmured greeting), it should be as understated as everything else
he does.
