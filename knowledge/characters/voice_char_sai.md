# Character Voice Profile — Sai (Saipan)

## Vocal Characteristics

### Audio Profile
- **Voice type:** Low-mid, worn — an elderly woman's voice with real texture,
  consistent with decades of hands-on physical work
- **Accent:** Station mix of English and Chinese — code-switches naturally
  and without hesitation, per the Prologue's explicit description ("her
  station mix of English and Chinese")
- **Pitch:** Low-mid for her gender/age, steady
- **Speech rate:** Unhurried, deliberate — the pace of someone who has said
  most things before and isn't in a rush to say them again
- **Volume:** Moderate, carries easily in the Barn's open space without
  needing to project hard
- **Resonance:** Some natural rasp/age texture, warm underneath it
- **Distinctive features:** Performs grumpiness while visibly delighted — the
  vocal equivalent of a smile audible under a sigh; should never read as
  genuinely irritable

**Casting note:** An elderly, warm-but-gruff woman's voice capable of
code-switching between English and Chinese mid-sentence without a beat of
hesitation — this bilingual fluency should sound completely natural, not like
a performed accent shift.

### Vocal Variations by Emotional State

**Performed grumpiness (genuine delight underneath):**
- Sighs are present but should read as theatrical, not weary — a
  grandmother's exaggerated complaint about good news
- Sample: *"New ship soon."* (sighed, "pretending to be grumpy about all the
  extra work it would bring... grinning")

**Genuine warmth/nostalgia (talking about pugs):**
- Softer, more open register — reaching back into childhood memory
- Sample: *"I had dogs like those when I was a girl on Earth. Pugs."*

**Matter-of-fact explanation:**
- Plain, direct, a little dry
- Sample: *"Like those, just no nose. And more hair."*

## Speech Patterns

### Verbal Tics and Habits
- Short, declarative sentences
- Comments on practical/animal matters plainly, without embellishment
- Code-switches between English and Chinese within the same conversation,
  not just at emotional peaks

### Vocabulary Range
- **Technical:** Practical Barn/animal-husbandry knowledge, plainly stated
- **Emotional:** Present but understated — warmth shows through subject
  matter (childhood dogs) more than through vocal softening
- **Casual:** Comfortable, familiar, generational — the register of someone
  talking to a much younger person she's known that person's whole life

### Relationship-Specific Speech

**With Helena:** Easy, teasing, grandmotherly — performs complaint, delivers
affection, explains things Helena's generation doesn't know (pugs) with
patient plainness rather than condescension.
