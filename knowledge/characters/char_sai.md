# Sai (Saipan)

## Summary

Sai — full name possibly Saipan, or "Sai" as a diminutive of a longer name not yet
established — is an elderly Barn worker on Fortuna Station, introduced in the
Prologue as one of Helena's regular morning-rounds stops. She works the morning
shift in the Barn, old enough to have grandchildren of dating age and to remember
Earth animals — specifically pugs — from her own childhood, which places her
emigration from Earth decades in the past. She and Helena have the easy, teasing
rapport of two people who have known each other Helena's entire life.

## The Barn Routine

Helena's rounds take her through the Barn every morning, where she tops off a
water line, scratches a headbutting goat, and finds Sai working. They trade
gossip while "grasping the leathery hand of Saipan" — a small physical detail
suggesting this is a regular greeting ritual, not a formal handshake. Sai has
clearly been doing hands-on Barn work for a very long time; her hands show it.

## The New Ship

On the morning of the Prologue, the *Falcon*'s impending arrival is Barn gossip.
Sai performs grumpiness about the extra work a new shipment of settlers and
livestock will bring — "New ship soon," sighed, pretending to be put out — while
visibly grinning the entire time. She is delighted, and not hiding it well, and
isn't really trying to. When she glances up at a chicken wedged in a ceiling
strut, her aside — "Maybe they didn't need more chickens" — is the same
performed-grump register.

She's especially taken with the news of "an actual dog" (Cerberus) aboard the
inbound ship — the first dog most of the station's residents have ever seen in
person, given the belt's general lack of pets. This gives her a reason to reach
back into her own childhood: she had dogs like that once, as a girl on Earth.
Pugs. She explains the breed to Helena, who has never heard of one, by pointing
at a screen showing Cerberus and Siren: "Like those, just no nose. And more
hair." — delivered in her characteristic mix of English and Chinese.

## Family

Sai has at least two grandchildren of an age to have dated Helena: a grandson,
and a granddaughter, Betty. Helena dated both. Sai only ever knew about the
grandson — Helena deliberately kept the relationship with Betty from her,
because Sai is established as "old school traditionalist," and Helena judged
correctly that this was not information Sai needed or would take well. The
Prologue frames this lightly, as one of many small kindnesses/discretions Helena
extended to the people in her life — not as a wound or a conflict, just a fact
about how Helena managed the people she loved on a very small station where
everyone already knows almost everything about everyone else.
