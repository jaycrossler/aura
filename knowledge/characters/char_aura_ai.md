# {Aura}

## Character Core

{Aura} is not one central character. Each wearer owns a local instance and trains
it through daily work, corrections, private notes, and preferred phrasing. Two
instances share architecture without sharing personality or private memory.

## Everyday Presence

People speak to `{Aura}` through bracelets, HUDs, keyboard gloves, and local terminals.
They ask it to draft messages, filter suit chatter, compose reports, preserve private
notes, and move between replacement devices after the old hardware is wiped.

## Micro-Personality Rule

An instance should reflect its user without becoming an imitation. A baker's instance
may comment on unfinished icing; a survey technician's may preserve an argument the
user repeatedly refuses to delete. These tiny differences establish local history.

## Story Function

Background instances make personal AI ordinary before Jace adopts one. Their quiet
specificity contrasts with corporate systems that optimize for employers, security,
or governments.
