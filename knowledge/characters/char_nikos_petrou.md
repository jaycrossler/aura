# Dr. Nikos Petrou

## Summary

Nikos Petrou is the *Victoria's* Greek astrophysicist and primary sensor-instrument
specialist. He maintains the high-energy particle arrays, long-baseline detectors,
and calibration systems used to study Vela Junior flux anomalies. His work provides
the instrumentation that first exposes the unexplained signal behind Sophia's
crossings, although he does not yet understand that connection.

## Relationships and Manner

Nikos and [[char_brandon_moreau]] are partners. They have served with Sophia for four
years. Nikos is the more openly amused of the two and does little to conceal that he
recognizes Sophia's "instrument calibration" explanation as a cover story.

He respects privacy but not denial. At Chapter 15, he accepts that Sophia will explain
her discovery when she has evidence she trusts. He and Brandon remain alert because
her fatigue and changed routines are obvious.

## Knowledge Boundary

At Chapter 15, Nikos does not know about the Astral. He knows that the *Victoria's*
flux instruments recorded a real anomaly and that Sophia's private work relates to it.
