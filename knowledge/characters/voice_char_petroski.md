## What the KB establishes

From `char_petroski.md`: *the only Russian man on Fortuna, a retired
orbital-structures PhD who rotates through customs.*

That single sentence is a complete casting brief, and unusually good one:

- **Russian**, and canonically the only one on the station. His accent is a
  genuine marker rather than colour, and it should be unmistakable in a cast
  that is otherwise American, Korean, German, French, Greek, Filipino, Chinese,
  and Hispanic-American.
- **Retired PhD in orbital structures**, now doing customs shifts. Overqualified
  and unbothered by it. He should sound like someone who finds the paperwork
  restful.
- **Rotates through customs**, meaning this is not his identity. He is not a
  bureaucrat; he is a structural engineer standing at a desk.

## Provisional direction

Older, unhurried, dry, Russian. Genuinely intelligent and completely
uninterested in demonstrating it. Warm underneath the procedure. The comedy
available here is an academic doing a clerical job well and without complaint.

  Russian on the station" detail.
