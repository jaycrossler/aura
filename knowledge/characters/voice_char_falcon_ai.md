# Character Voice Profile — {Falcon}

> **Light profile with a structural flag.** See the open flags above. {Falcon} is
> a canonically braces-named AI with no character file, which makes it the only
> AI in the series missing from the `char_*_ai.md` set.

## What the KB establishes

- {Falcon} is the *Falcon*'s majordomo AI. Author ruling, 2026-07-15.
- {Alex} rides aboard the *Falcon* in a **no-executive partition**, meaning
  {Alex} can speak and advise but cannot act on ship systems. {Falcon} can.
- **Maureen tolerates it.** That word is doing work. She is a captain nearing
  retirement who has a ship AI she has not warmed to and has not removed.

## Provisional direction

The important contrast is with {Alex}, because they share the ship in Ch01
through Ch07 and a listener needs to tell them apart immediately. {Alex} is
canonically flat, corporate, scrubbed of region, and scripted-empathetic.
{Falcon} should therefore be its opposite in at least one clear dimension.

Recommendation, offered as a recommendation: {Falcon} is **old ship software**.
Functional, a little dated, no empathy layer at all because nobody built one,
and completely without {Alex}'s upsell reflex. Where {Alex} is a polished
customer-service product, {Falcon} is a tool that came with the vessel. That
reading is consistent with "Maureen tolerates it" and gives the two AIs an
audible split without inventing personality traits.

**This is a proposal, not canon. Author ruling needed.**
