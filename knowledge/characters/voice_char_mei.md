# Character Voice Profile — Mei

Mei has very little on-page dialogue in Ch00-07 — this is itself
characterization (see `char_mei.md`: "unmemorable in a crowd when she wants to
be, and sharply present when she doesn't"), so this profile is deliberately
lighter than the others in this batch rather than padded to match their
length.

## Vocal Characteristics

### Audio Profile
- **Voice type:** Light alto, controlled
- **Accent:** General American (Montana cover background) with no
  distinguishing regional markers — flat in the sense of giving nothing away,
  not flat in the sense of dull
- **Pitch:** Medium, extremely even — she does not let pitch rise or fall in
  ways that reveal reaction
- **Speech rate:** Measured, slightly slower than average — she chooses words
  before releasing them
- **Volume:** Low-moderate; built to not carry, not to project
- **Resonance:** Light, front-placed
- **Distinctive features:** Says exactly as much as needed and stops; her
  established trait of being "sharply present when she doesn't [want to be
  unmemorable]" should be reserved for future scenes — nothing in Ch00-07
  calls for that register yet

**Casting note:** A controlled, unremarkable-by-design female voice — the
opposite of a distinctive "character voice." The performance should actively
resist being memorable, which is harder to cast than it sounds; avoid any
base voice with a strong, identifiable quirk.

### Vocal Variations by Emotional State

**Default (cover-consistent, understated):**
