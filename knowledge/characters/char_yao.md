# Yáo

## Summary

Yáo is one of Jin Lóng's mining crew — large, brutal-quiet, scarred, with
compressed-spring stillness and multiple healed fractures in his hands and jaw.
He has been with Jin the longest of anyone still living at Arc 1's start, and
was present at the original asteroid Tick-Maw encounter where Jin's fire
abilities first manifested. He has fought at Jin's side on every asteroid claim
since — one of the crew's two most experienced fighters against Astral fauna,
alongside Shi Gang.

Whether Yáo manifested any Will ability of his own during these asteroid hunts
is an open question (see below) — currently undocumented, unlike Jin's confirmed
[Fire Shaping].

## Death

Yáo dies during the swarm attack (S-01), saving a child named Mira. He sees her
in the path of a breach and does not hesitate. This death is written clean and
fast — no speech. Cerberus, who has refused to approach Mira for the entirety of
Arc 1, ends that avoidance immediately afterward and sits beside her. See
`char_mira.md` and `char_cerberus.md`.

## Physical (from visual profile cross-reference)

Large, scarred, brutal-quiet build. Compressed-spring stillness. Multiple healed
fractures in his hands and jaw, consistent with years of asteroid-side combat
against Astral fauna, predating the swarm fight.
