# {SpoX}

## Character Core

{SpoX} coordinates mining, survey analysis, and drone allocation. It treats words
as expensive payload and human intuition as an undocumented sensor fault.

## Micro-Personality

- Answers with a number before an explanation.
- Uses additional numbers when asked to elaborate.
- Separates measured fact from human concern, then dismisses the concern.
- Is usually correct inside its model and dangerously incurious outside it.

## Story Function

{SpoX} gives the colony a blunt operational voice. Its reliable calculations make
its blind spots harder to challenge when Astral effects enter the worksite.
