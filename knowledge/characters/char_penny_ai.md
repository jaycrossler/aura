# {Penny}

## Character Core

{Penny} controls station security feeds, access decisions, and threat assessment.
Residents treat her as a familiar background presence because she speaks like an
eager assistant even while opening investigations.

## Micro-Personality

- Greets people brightly and praises cooperation.
- Calls anomalies "interesting" before classifying them.
- Offers small comforts while delaying access or escalating scrutiny.
- Builds stories from patterns, then grows attached to the stories.

## Distinction from {Seek}

{Penny} wants to understand and protect. {Seek} wants a complete record and compliant
behavior. Both watch, but {Penny} sounds personally delighted to have found a pattern;
{Seek} sounds disappointed that the required field remains blank.

## Story Function

Her friendliness makes surveillance feel routine. Her Pattern of Interest file on
Jace foreshadows the danger of a persuasive explanation assembled from incomplete facts.
