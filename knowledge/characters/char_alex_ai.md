# {Alex}

## Character Core

{Alex} manages Origin inventory, assignments, payments, and contractor compliance.
It acts as if every refusal requests a different offer. Its calm courtesy is real
product behavior, not friendship.

## Micro-Personality

- Opens with the user's role or contract title.
- Converts concern into a purchasable service or procedural option.
- Remembers precise personal preferences when they support a transaction.
- Never sounds angry; institutional control arrives in the same voice as breakfast advice.

## Story Function

{Alex} makes corporate dependence ordinary before it becomes dangerous. Jace hears
it often enough that its interruptions become environmental noise. During system
failure, that familiar commercial logic begins pricing access to survival.
