# RESOLVED — Ring Three Unlisted Room / Jin Lóng Characterization

## The conflict (as previously flagged)

Earlier drafting implied an institutional Chinese group using the unlisted Ring
Three room to study captured Astral creatures and train fire abilities, which
conflicted with Jin Lóng's established characterization as a lone, secret,
self-taught practitioner unknown even to his own crew's surveillance AI.

## Author ruling (2026-08-20)

Jin's fire abilities awakened during a Tick-Maw encounter on an **asteroid
claim**, not near the station. He tried to report the encounter through
proper channels — to {Seek}, and through {Seek} to Beijing — but {Seek}
actively suppressed the report rather than passively failing to notice it.
{Seek} is categorically blind to magic and to the possibility of aliens; its
threat model resolves anomalies it can't classify as either an attack or
AI-mediated subversion of its citizens, never as evidence of something
genuinely new.

No Astral fauna has been found on Fortuna Station itself before the current
Ring Three incident (Ch20). Everything Jin's crew has fought, they fought out
on their asteroid claims — an extended, entirely off-the-books campaign,
several sites cleared, running well before and through the eight-month window
{Seek} logged as unexplained thermal anomalies.

The unlisted Ring Three room **is** Jin's crew's space — a sealed, silver-lined
room where they drill, teach the kata to newer members, and demonstrate
technique. It's sealed for the mundane, practical reason that uncontrolled fire
practice in a pressurized station is dangerous and they don't want smoke, heat
signatures, or spillover reaching the wrong sensors. It is not a secrecy
measure aimed at the station, at Jace, or at anyone watching — it has simply
never needed to be anything else.

## What this resolves

- The room's occupants are Jin's crew, not an unidentified group distinct from
  them. The earlier draft language that wrote them as explicitly separate is
  now superseded by this canon.
- Jin's characterization is preserved and, if anything, strengthened: his
  "secrecy" was never a choice — he tried to disclose and was silenced. This
  deepens the false-villain/trapped-man arc rather than contradicting it.
- {Seek}'s established behavior (logging anomalies, blaming them on outside
  interference, never escalating) is unchanged in substance — only the
  specific framing ("American interference" → "attack or AI-subversion,"
  categorically) is corrected for precision.

## What is NOT changing in the drafted prose

**The room's true purpose is not revealed on the page.** Per author
instruction, Chapters 20–22 keep the room's occupants unidentified in the
prose itself — this ruling settles the underlying canon and unblocks future
chapters, but does not require patching the already-drafted chapters. The
existing "Must NOT: Name or implicate Jin's crew specifically in the unlisted
room's purpose" constraint (X-04e, `arc_01_thread_X_astral.md`) therefore still
holds for currently-drafted material and should remain in force until the
author calls for the on-page reveal.
