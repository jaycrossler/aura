# Open Questions — Material Seeding Patches (2026-08-19)

## 1. Ch14's "market collapse" line ties to existing canon — confirm framing

The new Carlos dialogue leans on the already-established "Propellant Market
Collapse" disaster scenario in `spec_belt_transport_and_economy.md" ("a major
discovery floods the market with platinum-group metals; expected profits
vanish"). I treated that as license to have Carlos state the policy plainly,
rather than inventing new economic canon. If you'd rather keep the market
mechanics vaguer at this point in the story, this patch can be softened.

## 2. Five plants, no payoff shown here

None of these patches reference the Astral-blocking property — that's
deliberate, since Ch20-22 is where it's discovered in-story. If you want a
sixth touchpoint even earlier (Ch1-6, before Fortuna) let me know; I didn't
find a natural fit in what I searched, but didn't do an exhaustive pass on the
