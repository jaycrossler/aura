# Open Questions — Ch20/21 Revision (Ring Three encounter merge)

## 1. Old Ch21 back-half material (spine observation, timers, {Seek} alerts)

The previously staged Ch21 draft's second half used a different encounter shape:
spine-side observation, paper navigation with independent timers, doubled
corridor geometry, a copper tether defense, {Seek}'s contradictory security
alerts, and a red eye that tracked Jace specifically. The revised opening (this
session) replaces the encounter itself with the Ring Two photograph mission,
Sparky-thruster fight, and silver stairway. The two don't fit together as
written.

**Decision needed:** Does any of the old material (doubled geometry, timer
failures, {Seek}'s contradictory alerts, the eye tracking Jace specifically) get
preserved and moved to a later chapter (22 or beyond), or is it retired in favor
of the new sequence? The {Seek} contradictory-alert beat in particular ties into
N-03 (AI degradation plant) and may still be needed somewhere.

## 2. Sophia's cut morning-after speech (from old Ch20 ending)

The old Ch20 ending carried Sophia's boundary-setting speech ("I am not sorry I
sent you away... I am afraid of the thing on Three...") along with the
unresolved Nick-apology beat. Both were built around the old Misty-observation
scene, which no longer happens in Ch20. The emotional content (Sophia's fear,
Jace withholding facts from Nick) is worth keeping.

**Decision needed:** Fold into Chapter 22's recovery material, or rework into a
Ch21 aftermath scene after the bunk/Kim beat? Recommend Ch22, since Ch21 as
revised is already carrying the fight, the drain, and the bunk scene, and
Sophia's fuller emotional reckoning may land better once both of them have slept.

## 3. Parasite implantation as a host-locating mechanism (new lore, not yet canon)

Jay proposed that the Tick-Maw bite/egg mechanism (already canonical per X-02a)
exists specifically as a way for Tick-Maws to locate more hosts. This is a
plausible extension but is not stated anywhere in the current KB. Per session
agreement, this is being planted silently in Ch20's attack (the wound, not the
mechanism) and NOT stated as dialogue or theory on-page. Flagging here so it
doesn't get treated as settled canon until Jay confirms it explicitly for the
species-behavior file (no such file currently exists for Tick-Maws as a species;
recommend creating one if this is confirmed).

## 4. Tick-Maw seeding mechanism — same as Ch9 miner, or parallel

Jay confirmed in this session: yes, same mechanism as the Ch9 intact-suit miner
wound (per X-02a). Logging here as a decision already made, not open, so it
doesn't get re-litigated later.
