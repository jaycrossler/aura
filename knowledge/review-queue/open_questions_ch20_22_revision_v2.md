## 1. Chinese secret room — fire training and monster study (MAJOR, needs your ruling)

See the full flag in `arcs/patch_arc_01_thread_X_astral_v2.md`. Short version:
Jay's request implies an institutional Chinese group using the unlisted room to
study captured Astral creatures and train fire abilities. This conflicts with
Jin Lóng's established characterization as a lone, secret, self-taught
practitioner unknown even to his own crew's oversight AI. This session's
chapters wrote the room's occupants as unidentified and explicitly distinct
from Jin's known crew, to avoid contradicting existing canon while still
delivering the beat Jay asked for. **Needs explicit confirmation this is the
intended reading**, or a different resolution if the connection to Jin was
deliberate.
