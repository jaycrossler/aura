# Chapter 19. Fault Lines

> *Most people do not know this, but a fault line is not a crack in the rock. It is two places that used to agree about where they were going.*
>
> Jace Grant, *unpublished notes*

## 1. Cautious Astral Sessions

Jace and Sophia establish strict new operational rules following the rescue behind Fortuna.

- Crossing protocols enforced: short duration only, no open-space travel, independent timers, and paper mapping routes.
- Jace adheres to caution in the Astral while continuing to avoid Nick in physical station life.

## 2. The K-17 Silt Survey

Jace operates drone surveys over the K-17 asteroid sector.

- Drone telemetry detects deep silt beds and structural instability beneath the proposed station expansion zone.
- Jace recognizes that physical sensors capture only a fraction of the underlying structural weakness.

## 3. Political Tension and the Long Burn Fight

Station news feeds amplify accusations between American administration and Chinese sector mining teams.

- At the Long Burn bar, Chinese miner Yáo confronts Jace over drone reroutes, delayed oxygen shipments, and lost shift credit.
- Defensive and stressed, Jace exchanges sharp words that escalate into a physical brawl.
- Jin Lóng intervenes to stop the fight, refusing the sabotage narrative while enforcing order and making Jace leave.
