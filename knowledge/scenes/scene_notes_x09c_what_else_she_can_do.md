# X-09c — What Else She Can Do

**Placement:** Follows X-09b. Builds on the already-contracted X-03b ("What
She Can Do," physical side) with a more detailed, self-aware pass — Sophia
isn't just demonstrating anymore, she's trying to explain the mechanism to
herself out loud, with Jace as sounding board.

## The pen

Sophia's father's wood pen (with its removable metal nib — see her character
file) lifts a few centimeters off the desk, wobbling, while she stares at it
with the specific strained stillness of someone holding a held breath.

> "Superconductors have rows of atoms packed in exactly the right pattern. If
> those atoms are warm and vibrating, all the signals get jumbled. Keep them
> cold and it locks the pattern in place — no jumbling — and that lets it
> push off a magnetic field." She doesn't look away from the pen. "If I think
> just the right way, I think I'm making the same thing happen. My pen is
> lifting because I'm stopping the vibrations. Somehow." The pen drops the
> instant she says it. "If I stop thinking that way, it just falls."

## The robot

Later, more controlled, she demonstrates on a maintenance bot rolling past:

> "I can push that robot over. Just a bit. If I go like—" *(a specific,
> small gesture — TBD blocking)* The bot rocks, corrects, keeps rolling.
> "Somehow my thoughts are making it change its patterns."

Jace tries to replicate what she's doing and can't — not the pen, not the
bot. This should read as a real character beat, not a joke: his mind doesn't
work this way, and it needles him a little that hers does. He's not
resentful, but he notices the asymmetry directly here for the first time —
this is the character-level echo of the still-open "why 10x" question from
X-09/X-09b, felt rather than explained.

## The sky

During a session where they're both physical-side, looking up (through a
viewport, or from the surface — placement TBD), Sophia notices something
that has nothing to do with either of their abilities directly:

> "Do you see that? Outside — there seem to be clouds. Through space." She
> keeps watching. "If you look at one star, or the sun, long enough, they
> flicker. And Earth is somehow brighter than the sun. I don't know why. You
> can almost see Mars too. It's closer right now, but it should be invisible
> — too dark. It's like they're all closer for some reason. They don't glow
> right."

This should land as unsettling rather than explained — a first hint that
whatever is changing about the Astral is not confined to Fortuna, or even to
the solar system's interior. Do not resolve it here. Cross-reference
`spec_astral_phenomenology.md` — flag for that file if it doesn't already
carry a note on naked-eye sky anomalies from physical space.

**Must accomplish:**
1. Sophia's superconductor analogy is offered as her own working hypothesis,
   not confirmed physics — she's guessing with real rigor, not lecturing
2. Jace's inability to replicate her tricks is shown, not stated as a moral —
   a beat of quiet frustration, not comedy
3. The sky-flicker/Earth-brighter-than-sun/Mars-visible observation is planted
   as unexplained and slightly wrong, with no resolution offered
4. Keep all three beats small and habitual — these read as things Sophia
   notices during ordinary work sessions, not a single big reveal scene

**Must NOT do:**
- Explain the superconductor mechanism as literally true — it's Sophia's
  analogy, useful but unverified
- Resolve the sky anomaly
- Use `[Skill]` bracket notation (pre-convention)

**Foreshadowing planted:**
- Sky phenomena (flicker, Earth over-bright, Mars visibly too close) — series-
  level thread, likely tied to the wider Astral/Hyperspace changes referenced
  in Open Decision #8b (X-10, rotating mirror). Do not connect them explicitly
  yet; let them sit as two separate unexplained anomalies until the author
  decides whether to merge them.
