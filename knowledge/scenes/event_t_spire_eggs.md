# Scene Contract — "The Spire"

Bridge chapter between the plumbing-crisis incursion and the Fortuna Astral
discovery. Purpose: show more of the Astral pressing in, raise tension across
the final two weeks of transit, and seed the everyone-gets-Will thread via
Lanchee — all without explaining anything.

## Must accomplish (testable)

1. **Post-orb changes shown, never explained.** Headaches gone since the
   plumbing crisis (Jace credits stress resolution). Crystal dreams now vivid
   and nightly. First waking perception bleed: intermittent, headache-inducing
   flashes of seeing *through* things — shapes beyond a wall that HUD overlays
   and majordomo cameras flatly contradict. The bleed costs him (fresh
   pressure behind the eyes, distinct from the old HUD ache) so it reads as a
   symptom, not a superpower.
2. **The spire climb.** Movement glimpsed toward the rarely-traveled central
   spine. Cerberus can't do the stairs; stays below, barking nervously —
   audible dread the whole climb. The low-spin spire is dizzying; the vertigo
   and the perception bleed blur into each other (deniability preserved).
3. **The panel and the eggs.** Drill out an access panel. His Astral-bleed
   sight shows two small leathery eggs; HUD shows an empty cavity. As he
   nears, the eggs crack — two hatchling Dippers jump out and attack.
4. **The fight.** Physical, close, ugly. Jace kills one with the drill through
   the head — it bursts in a shimmer and he absorbs a second Will orb without
   registering what happened. The smaller hatchling leaps straight through the
   bulkhead into space and is gone. Do not name orbs, Dippers, or the Astral.
5. **Institutional blindness, and Jace managing it.** He reports movement to
   {Alex}; the majordomo's cameras show only Jace flailing at nothing with a
   powered drill. {Alex} fines him for dangerous tool operation near complex
   equipment. Jace is now actively wary: two fines into a five-year debt
   contract, he cannot afford to be profiled as a risk. He starts choosing
   what *not* to report — the private anomaly file becomes deliberate
   concealment, not just habit.
6. **Lanchee beat (seed, do not flag).** Treating the deep arm cut, Jace asks
   if she's noticed anything weird aboard. She laughs it off: only that her
   memory has been *remarkably* sharp — few patients, the silence of space,
   "a vacation for the mind." Then, looking at the wound before scanning it,
   she names the torn nerve and injured tendon exactly — and is briefly,
   visibly surprised her guess was exactly right. She moves on. (Canon: this
   is an early Will manifestation — diagnostic intuition + recall. Payoff
   later; foreshadows that everyone will begin manifesting.)
7. **Aftermath dread.** Jace patrols the full ship daily for the remaining
   two weeks. Nothing. The absence is worse than a sighting.

## Must NOT

- Explain orbs, the Astral, or what Lanchee's accuracy means
- Let anyone believe Jace, or let Jace fully believe himself
- Let the escaped hatchling reappear before Fortuna
- Make the fight heroic — it's frightening, clumsy, and over fast
- {Alex} as villain: the fine is procedure, cheerfully administered

## Emotional beat

Unease → vertiginous wrongness → violence and adrenaline → gaslighting by
every instrument he trusts → the loneliness of being the only witness.
Reader leaves certain; Jace leaves at 60% and falling.
