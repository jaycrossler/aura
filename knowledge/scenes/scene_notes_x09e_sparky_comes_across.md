# X-09e — Sparky Comes Across

**Placement:** Follows X-09d, overlapping in time. Sparky's physical-side
mobility work (rolling, then hovering) is a separate, ongoing project thread
that reaches a milestone here and gets folded into the Astral work once it
does.

## Physical side

Jace finally gets Sparky rolling, then floating — small thruster work,
incremental, satisfying, mostly off-page or covered briefly (cross-reference
whatever workshop/Sparky material already exists in Ch08–10 rather than
re-litigating it here).

## Crossing him over, piece by piece

Once Sparky can move under his own power, Jace starts pulling him across
using the same rebuild-and-transfer logic from X-09d, but this time
transferring actual components rather than rebuilding from scratch — Sparky
is a specific object with sentimental and functional continuity, not a
sensor he can print twice.

The mini complex thruster engine is the hard part: dense, alloy-heavy,
tightly toleranced. **Two hours of heavy, exhausted pulling** for one small
engine — this should read as real physical and mental cost, consistent with
the atomic-weight rule established in X-09b (complex alloyed electronics are
expensive to cross).

The shell is easy by comparison — simple metal, low complexity, crosses in
a fraction of the time and effort.

Spread across several days and sessions, Jace gets enough of Sparky across to
reassemble him on the Astral side. Once whole again, Sparky becomes a working
tool for their experiments there — an extra set of hands/sensors, not a
combat asset, not dramatic. The reunion beat (Sparky powering on, first roll
across Astral terrain) can be small and a little funny rather than a big
emotional set piece — Jace's relief is quiet and practical.

**Must accomplish:**
1. The thruster engine's cost (two hours, exhausting) is shown as directly
   consistent with the atomic-weight rule from X-09b — heavy, complex,
   alloyed components cost more
2. The shell's cost is shown as a clean contrast — simple material, fast
   crossing
3. The reassembly is a multi-day process, not a single scene — texture
   across this stretch, referenced rather than shown blow-by-blow after the
   first crossing or two
4. Sparky's Astral-side utility is established (helps with experiments) but
   kept modest — a tool, not a new character beat requiring its own arc

**Must NOT do:**
- Turn Sparky's reassembly into a single dramatic action sequence
- Contradict the atomic-weight rule — every crossing-cost detail here should
  reinforce X-09b, not introduce a new unexplained mechanic
- Use `[Skill]` bracket notation (pre-convention)
