# The American Red Party

## Summary

The American Red party is the political faction that takes control of the U.S. federal
government roughly six months after Jace refuses the bridge strike. It is
hyper-capitalist, unsentimental, and deeply invested in AI: it uses AI to administer,
optimize, and govern at scale, and it prizes **speed, growth, and control** above the
slower virtues of deliberation and dissent.

It is written as a coherent actor, **not a cartoon villain.** From inside its own frame
it is not doing evil; it is removing friction. An institution that cannot move quickly
when told to move is, to the American Red, a liability — and it treats liabilities
accordingly. The series' "no clean villains" pillar applies here in full: the party's
logic is internally consistent and, on its own terms, defensible, even as its human cost
is real and borne by sympathetic people.

## Core Doctrine

- **Speed over deliberation.** Hesitation is waste; a chain of command that pauses to
  argue is a broken chain.
- **Growth over stability.** Expansion is the metric; the belt and Mars are assets to be
  optimized, not communities to be tended.
- **Control over trust.** Loyalty is verified, not assumed — preferably by systems that
  don't get tired, sentimental, or insubordinate. Hence the heavy reliance on AI.

## Visual Identity and Fortuna Presence

The party uses a red-washed version of the American flag with fifty-four stars and a giant red flag bearing an eagle as its public symbols. Official portraits show the party's large, jowled leader wearing a red hat. Fortuna's customs area displays these images behind the American desk, where the rotating officer formally says, "On behalf of the American Red Party, welcome to Fortuna."

The American mural deliberately sits beside a Chinese red flag and a similarly framed portrait of China's round-faced leader. The similarity is politically uncomfortable. Carlos reduces it to, "Different color hair," while Petroski snorts and leaves. The scene introduces the party through civic ritual, visual control, and station humor before its role in Jace's purge receives a full explanation.

## The Purge

On taking power, the administration ran a mass, AI-administered discharge of military
personnel judged unreliable or slow to follow orders. Discharges were processed
administratively, at scale, in bulk — **thousands in a single day.** Charges were often
trumped-up hooks hung on real incidents.

Jace is one of these: dishonorably discharged on an insubordination charge built on his
refusal to strike the bridges — a refusal his own supervisor (Major Barnes) had backed
months earlier. To the party this is housekeeping, not malice. The mission he sank was
expensive and real; the rule that he broke was real; the administration simply decided
that people who break that rule are the ones it can do without. (Jace is barred from
discussing the underlying mission; it is sealed.)

## Relationship to AI and {Eagle}

The American Red governs through AI and extends that instinct into strategic
infrastructure. It is the party that covertly deploys **{Eagle}**, a federal overwatch
intelligence, onto Fortuna Station — hidden, unregistered, riding beside the station's
other AIs and quietly holding more authority than any of them know. {Eagle} is the
party's doctrine in software: a loyal, tireless guardian enforcing control with complete
confidence in its own sensors. (See `tech_eagle_ai.md`; reader-only in Arc 1.)

## Relationship to Origin and Mars

- **Origin Industries:** the party's hyper-capitalist, AI-heavy character is congenial to
  Origin's corporate doctrine; how far Origin actively cooperates (e.g., federal
  backdoors in Origin hardware that let {Eagle} in) is an open question tied to `tech_eagle_ai.md`.
- **U.S. Mars:** Mars Command is canonically drifting into autonomy, "isolated from
  Washington by comms lag and political drift." This lets pockets of the old guard (e.g.,
  a Sylvester-type intelligence holdout) operate quietly out of step with the Red
  administration — a tension worth mining. (Fills the open "current governor's party"
  question in `faction_united_states_mars.md` — confirm on apply.)
